# Privacy Policy for Tilx

**Last updated:** October 6, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect
Tilx ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. We are committed to protecting your personal information. Our application operates primarily on your device.
- **Local Storage:** All article history and generated social media cards are saved locally on your device using a local SQLite database. We do not sync this data to our servers.
- **Article Metadata:** When you submit a URL to generate a card, the URL is sent to our cloud function solely to extract Open Graph metadata (title, description, image). We do not log, store, or associate these URLs with your personal identity.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
The information processed by our cloud function is used strictly in real-time to generate the visual content for your social media cards.

## 3. Third-Party Services
We use third-party services (such as Firebase for cloud functions and Expo for app analytics). These services may collect information used to identify you in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
